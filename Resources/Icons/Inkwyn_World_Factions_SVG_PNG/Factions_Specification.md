# Inkwyn — Runic Whimsy
## World Object Glyph: Factions v0.1

**Recognition anchor:** A heraldic shield bearing crossed antique keys.

**Construction:** A substantial symmetric heater-like shield with an organic pointed lower tip and flared upper contours. Two antique keys cross diagonally through its center; distinctive open bows and notched bits keep them recognizable as keys rather than an X. The Large design carries the approved decorative scrollwork and four-pointed diamond ornaments.

**Meaning:** Collective identity, membership, shared authority or allegiance. Applicable to guilds, groups, institutions, alliances and factions without implying an exclusively military character.

**Optical reduction:** Large reproduces the detailed approved design; Medium reduces small decorative flourishes and minor contours; Small prioritizes the shield silhouette, crossed shafts, open key bows and clearly notched key bits.

**Submenu indicator:** None. World Object glyphs identify object types, not menus.

**Production:** Monochrome SVG; transparent 512 × 512 PNG rendered from corresponding SVG.

**Status:** Concept approved; optical SVG/PNG variants submitted for review.
