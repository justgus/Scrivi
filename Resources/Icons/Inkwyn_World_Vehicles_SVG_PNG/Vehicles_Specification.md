# Inkwyn — Runic Whimsy
## World Object Glyph: Vehicles v0.1

**Recognition anchor:** Celtic horse-headed Viking longship prow.

**Construction:** A right-facing horse profile forms the carved figurehead of a rising ship prow. Pointed ears, an angular muzzle, a prominent eye, and curling mane are integrated into broad Celtic interlace. The interlace descends into a curved prow and an abbreviated sweep of hull; stylized waves signal a voyage. The horse is deliberately part of the ship's carved structure rather than an animal standing aboard it.

**Meaning:** Represents any vehicle or means of conveyance in an Inkwyn World, including land, sea, air, space, magical, or invented transport. The metaphor alludes to horses, maritime voyaging, and Poseidon's association with both.

**Optical reduction:**
- **Large:** Preserve the horse's full profile, layered mane, Celtic interlace, sweeping prow, hull, and wave treatment.
- **Medium:** Reduce thin interior strands and woodcut details while retaining the horse profile, interlaced neck, rising prow, and hull.
- **Small:** Strengthen essential ink contours, simplify intricate knotwork, and prioritize the horse head and distinctive bow silhouette.

**Constraints:** No separate submenu marker; monochrome tintable shape; transparent background. The glyph must not reduce to a standalone chess knight or be confused with the full longboat in Import/Export.

**Reference:** Approved Celtic Horse-Prow concept (right-hand sketch), not the naturalistic version. Assets are vector tracings of that concept; fine paper texture and hairline ink strokes are omitted for legibility.

**Status:** Concept selected; assets provided for review.
