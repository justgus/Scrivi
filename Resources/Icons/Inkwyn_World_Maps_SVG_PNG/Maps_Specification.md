# Inkwyn — Runic Whimsy
## World Object Glyph: Maps v0.1

**Recognition anchor:** A familiar three-panel folded cartographic map.

**Construction:** Three folding parchment panels with an articulated, gently undulating top and bottom edge. A prominent central four-point compass rose and runic diamond are accompanied by a winding river or route, terrain mountains, and a destination X. Decorative curls in the Large form recall rolled parchment corners. The geometry remains unmistakably a folded map rather than a narrative Location threshold or a Navigate compass.

**Meaning:** Represents a map object within a World, a depiction of places and their spatial relationships, without constraints on geography, medium, or setting.

**Optical reduction:**
- **Large:** Full approved tri-fold, curl details, mountains, trees, compass star, river/route, dotted journey and marked destination.
- **Medium:** Simplified folds, terrain, compass and route, without elaborate corner curls.
- **Small:** Essential three-panel silhouette, compass diamond, reduced mountains, route and endpoint.

**Submenu indicator:** None.
**Color and background:** Monochromatic black, transparent background; tintable by UI.
**Production:** Each SVG was separately traced from the approved optical-size concept and corresponding PNG rendered from that SVG. Minor contour differences from the concept rendering are possible.
**Status:** Concept approved; optical SVG/PNG artwork submitted for review.
