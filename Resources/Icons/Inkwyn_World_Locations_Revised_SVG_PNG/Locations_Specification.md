# Inkwyn — Runic Whimsy
## Locations: Narrative Threshold — Revised Optical Set v0.2

**Recognition anchor:** A freestanding arched portal, a narrative threshold into a named setting.

**Meaning:** A World Location is a named setting significant to a story: a room, ship's bridge, forest, street, planet, or any other place, without constraints on kind or scale.

**Construction:** Gothic-inspired peaked arch, open inner passage, paired columns and grounded step. The runic diamond crowns the opening, with curved organic stonework and carved uprights. It denotes entrance to a setting, distinct from the Viking-hall Building glyph.

**Optical variants:**
- **Large:** The approved ornate portal silhouette, including the runic crown ornament, layered arch, column carvings, climbing vines, and masonry/grass grounding.
- **Medium:** Removes vines and grass while retaining the nested arch, pillar carvings, diamond crown, and broad step.
- **Small:** Reinforced portal arch and columns, with most inner ornament removed; retain a tiny diamond at the arch crown.

**Submenu indicator:** None.
**Asset approach:** Monochrome vector tracing of the corresponding revised concept sheet drawings; the PNG images are rendered from the SVG files with transparent backgrounds. Minor tracing deviations are possible. 
**Status:** Revised optical candidates for review; not approved production assets.
