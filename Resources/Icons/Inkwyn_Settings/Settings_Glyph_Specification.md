# Inkwyn — Runic Whimsy Settings Utility Glyphs (Review v0.1)

**Status:** Review candidates, not yet approved.

## App Settings
Recognition anchor: standalone organically shaped gear with eight outward teeth and a four-point diamond in the center. Application-wide settings and preferences.
- Large: full sculpted tooth curvature and diamond hub.
- Medium: simplified gear, open hub, four-point diamond.
- Small: same four-point diamond motif; reduce surrounding detail, not semantic identity.

## Project Settings
Recognition anchor: open manuscript/codex as the dominant silhouette, with an overlapping small gear at lower right. Settings of the current writing project.
- Large: multiple curved page lines and carved folds; prominent overlapping cog.
- Medium: fewer page lines and simplified cog.
- Small: bold two-page open book with clear central diamond/fold and smaller cog; maintain silhouette separation.

## Production conventions
- Six independent SVGs, with exact-image traced vector contours from the reviewed concept art. Each SVG uses `currentColor`, transparent background, even-odd fill.
- Six matching 512×512 transparent PNGs rendered in near-black #191513.
- Optically differentiated Large, Medium, Small versions, not resizes of one master.
- These are close contour reconstructions of approved-to-review visual concepts rather than hand-authored, optimized Bézier masters. Inspect small-scale rendering before approval.
- No right-hand submenu diamonds; both glyphs represent direct settings actions.
