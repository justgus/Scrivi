# Inkwyn — Runic Whimsy: Zoom to Current Scene

**Concept:** Focus Pip (A). **Status:** Selected concept, optical assets for review; not yet approved.

**Meaning:** Center and zoom the timeline on its current scene.

**Recognition anchor:** Solid scene pip surrounded by four disconnected runic viewfinder corners, plus four diamond cardinal markers. A framing/action glyph, intentionally distinct from the circular scene-aggregation ring.

**Large:** Organic, gently flared corner geometry, four runic diamonds, solid central disc.

**Medium:** Simplify corner terminals; retain separate diamonds and strengthen disc.

**Small:** Broaden the framing components and central disc; enlarge diamonds to avoid disappearance at 16–20 pt.

**Design constraints:** Single monochrome fill; no background, arrow, lens, text, aggregate outer ring, or submenu marks. Preserve symmetry and negative space between all parts.

**Assets:** 3 independent SVG optical masters using `currentColor`, 3 transparent PNG at 512 × 512 pixels; a review preview with light/dark toolbar-size checks. SVGs are editable vector geometry, not embedded raster images.
