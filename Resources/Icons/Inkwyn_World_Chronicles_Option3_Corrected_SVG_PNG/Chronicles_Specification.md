# Inkwyn — Runic Whimsy: Chronicles

**Approved source:** Option 3, Ceremonial Scroll, from the angled-scroll comparison sheet. The previous package inadvertently traced Option 2 and is superseded.

**Recognition anchor:** An angled ceremonial parchment scroll with two pronounced cylindrical rollers and projecting pointed finials on BOTH ends of BOTH rollers.

**Construction:** Dark monochrome vector trace of the chosen three-quarter-view ceremonial scroll. Thick, layered bands at the roller fittings; curled depth on the right ends; an organic suspended parchment sheet with angular edges; a diamond rune in the upper center; two curved inscription marks. The finials and ornament distinguish this form from the ordinary inscribed scroll Option 2.

**Meaning:** Any narrative record in a World, frequently a historical account but not limited to history or chronological entries.

**Large:** Preserves full roller, finial, bands and inscription contours.

**Medium:** Simplifies minor contours, retains all four finials, bands, diamond, inscriptions, angled presentation.

**Small:** Removes fine micro-details while maintaining the four projecting finials, roller bodies, diamond and recognizable scroll silhouette.

**Submenu indicator:** None.

**Output:** Three SVG variants and three corresponding 512x512 transparent PNG images, with the reference crop included. SVGs are automatically traced from the approved raster, not hand-drawn reconstructions; minor contour variance is possible.
